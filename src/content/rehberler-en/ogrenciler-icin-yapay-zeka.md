AI assistants are a double-edged tool for students. Used well, they can explain topics like a patient private tutor; used badly, they can replace learning and hide gaps in knowledge. This guide explains how to use AI in ways that strengthen your learning.

## 1. Learn the rules

First, find out your school's and teachers' AI policy. In some courses AI is completely banned, in others it is allowed for certain tasks, and in others it is permitted as long as you disclose its use. If you are not sure, ask.

## 2. Ask for the explanation, not the answer

Instead of "What is the answer to this question?", say "Give me the first step for solving this question as a hint, but don't tell me the answer." You can put the assistant into "Socratic tutor" mode: "Don't give me the answer; ask me questions so I can work it out myself." This approach helps you use what you've learned on the exam.

## 3. Have it explain topics you don't understand in different ways

If you didn't understand a topic from your textbook, ask for explanations at different levels: "Explain photosynthesis first to a 10-year-old, then at a high school level." Ask for everyday analogies and examples. Compare what it tells you with your class notes.

## 4. Test yourself

Ask the assistant to prepare questions on the topic you are studying: "Prepare a 10-question multiple-choice test on the founding period of the Ottoman Empire; give the answers at the end." It is even more effective to give it your class notes and ask it to generate questions from them.

## 5. Get feedback on what you write

Submit your own homework and say, "Don't rewrite this for me; show me the weak points in my argument, missing evidence and problems with my writing." That way the text stays yours, but gets better.

## 6. Verify information and sources

AI can produce confident-sounding wrong information and sources that don't exist. Verify every piece of information you use in your assignments against your textbook, an academic database or a reliable source. Don't add a source suggested by the assistant to your bibliography without actually finding and reading it.

## 7. Make a study plan

You can give the assistant your exam dates and topics and have it prepare a realistic study plan. Details such as "My math exam is in 10 days, I'm responsible for these topics, and I can set aside 1.5 hours a day" personalize the plan.

## Quick rules

- Use AI to support your thinking, not to replace it.
- You should be able to explain every sentence you submit.
- When it is permitted, state in your assignment how you used AI.
- Don't share your personal information or other people's information.
