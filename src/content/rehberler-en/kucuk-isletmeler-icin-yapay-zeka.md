AI isn't just for big companies. A café, an accounting firm, an e-commerce store or a freelance designer can save hours every week with ready-made AI assistants. The key is choosing the right task and starting carefully.

## 1. Pick the task that takes the most time

For one week, note down your repetitive, time-consuming tasks: replying to customer emails, writing product descriptions, preparing quotes, posting on social media, taking meeting notes. Try AI on one of these first.

## 2. Speed up customer communication

You can have AI draft reply templates for frequently asked questions, polite rejection and delay notices, campaign announcements, and responses to customer complaints. Edit the drafts to match your business's tone and save them. For more details, see our [guide to writing emails with AI](/rehber/yapay-zeka-ile-e-posta-yazma/).

## 3. Produce marketing content

Product descriptions, website copy, social media posts, and brochure and poster text are among the things AI does best. Give it the product's real features and avoid exaggerated claims. See [creating social media content](/rehber/yapay-zeka-ile-sosyal-medya-icerigi-uretme/).

## 4. Use it for quotes and documents

You can get a first draft from AI for quotes, business plan drafts, simple contract drafts and presentations. Always have legal and financial documents checked by a specialist; AI can misstate current legislation.

## 5. Work with data and numbers

You can use AI to analyze your sales tables, find your best-selling products, write Excel formulas and prepare simple reports. See the [guide to Excel and data analysis](/rehber/yapay-zeka-ile-excel-ve-veri-analizi/). Remove customer names and personal information before uploading.

## 6. Learn and get advice

You can use AI as a thinking partner for things like entering a new market, setting a pricing strategy or understanding an incentive program. Verify current legislation, tax and incentive information with official sources and your financial advisor.

## 7. Consider automation and chatbots later

Once you have experience with ready-made assistants, you can move on to more advanced steps such as adding a chatbot to your website, automatically sorting incoming emails or connecting your appointment system with AI. With systems like these, it's important that they answer based on your own documents (RAG) and hand off to a human when they make a mistake.

## Risks and precautions

- **Data privacy:** Don't enter customer information into general-purpose tools; keep your obligations under KVKK (Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law) in mind. See the [guide to protecting personal data](/rehber/yapay-zeka-kullanirken-kisisel-verileri-koruma/).
- **Incorrect information:** Check every piece of information that goes out to customers.
- **Over-reliance:** Treat AI as an assistant; the customer relationship and the decisions should stay with you.
