Unlike traditional translation tools, AI assistants can understand a translation's purpose, target audience and tone. That makes far more natural results possible, as long as you explain clearly what you want.

## 1. State the purpose and target audience

Instead of "Translate this into English," say "Translate this product description into English for American customers; use plain, sales-oriented language." An academic article, a marketing text and a user manual should each be translated very differently.

## 2. Set the tone and form of address

Turkish distinguishes between formal "siz" and informal "sen," and German and French have similar differences in formality. Specify which form of address to use in the target language and whether the text should be formal or friendly.

## 3. Provide a glossary

If you have industry-specific terms or brand names, give a short list: "Translate 'kampanya' as 'promotion' and 'bayi' as 'dealer'; do not translate product names." This ensures consistency, especially in long translations done in multiple parts.

## 4. Say whether you want adaptation or a literal translation

In marketing copy, idioms, jokes and cultural references need to be adapted to the target culture (localization). In technical and legal texts, preserving the exact meaning is what matters. Specify which one you want.

## 5. Split long documents into sections

With very long documents, the assistant may skip or shorten some sentences. It is more reliable to have the document translated in sections and check that the paragraph count stays the same in each one.

## 6. Have the translation checked

After receiving the translation, you can ask in a new chat: "Compare this translation with the source text; are there any shifts in meaning, missing sentences or terminology inconsistencies?" Having someone who knows the target language do a final check is always best.

## 7. Try back-translation

If you don't know the target language, you can have the translation translated back into Turkish to see whether the meaning has been preserved. This method catches major errors but does not reveal subtle style problems.

## When do you need a professional translator?

- Official documents, court and notary procedures
- Contracts and legal correspondence
- Medical reports and drug information
- Books to be published and important brand campaigns

For these kinds of texts, the safest approach is to use AI as a first draft or comprehension tool and leave the final text to an expert.
