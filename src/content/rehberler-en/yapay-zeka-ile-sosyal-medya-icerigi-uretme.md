Producing regular, high-quality social media content is one of the most time-consuming tasks for small businesses and creators. AI assistants can help at every stage of the process, from finding ideas and writing copy to generating visuals and building a calendar.

## 1. Define your brand voice

Tell the assistant about your brand, your target audience and your tone: "A third-wave coffee shop in Kadıköy. Target audience is 20-35 years old, urban, quality-conscious. Our tone is friendly and witty but not over the top; we use few emojis." Giving 2-3 of your past posts that you like as examples makes it easier to nail the tone.

## 2. Generate content ideas

Start with a prompt such as: "Suggest 20 content ideas for next month, grouped as informative, entertaining, behind-the-scenes and promotional." Also mention special occasions, the season and what's in the news in your industry. Pick the most suitable ideas and discard the rest.

## 3. Have it write copy for each platform

Every platform has its own language. LinkedIn calls for a professional, experience-driven tone; Instagram for short copy supported by visuals; X for concise, attention-grabbing writing. Use prompts that specify the platform, such as: "Turn this idea into a 150-word LinkedIn post that opens with a personal experience." Ask for several alternatives for the first sentence so it grabs attention.

## 4. Prepare a short-video script

For Reels, TikTok and Shorts: "Write a 30-second video script promoting this product; include an attention-grabbing hook in the first 3 seconds, scene-by-scene shooting suggestions and on-screen text." Adapt the script to your own filming capabilities.

## 5. Generate visuals or get visual ideas

With image-generation tools you can create post visuals, backgrounds or illustrations. For product photos, however, showing the real product is better for building trust. For tips on writing good image prompts, see our [image creation guide](/rehber/yapay-zeka-ile-gorsel-olusturma/).

## 6. Build a content calendar

Create a calendar by asking: "Distribute these 12 posts over 4 weeks, with 3 posts per week; give me the date, platform, content type and visual needs in a table." You can transfer the table to your spreadsheet and share it with your team.

## 7. Prepare draft replies to comments

You can have the assistant draft replies to frequently asked questions and negative comments. However, a human should always do the final check in customer communication; an automated, incorrect reply can damage your reputation.

## Things to watch out for

- Do not share statistics and claims written by AI without verifying them against the source.
- Do not generate visuals that imitate the likeness of real people, celebrities or other brands.
- Do not copy competitors' slogans or content.
- Personal experience, real customer stories and behind-the-scenes content provide an originality that AI cannot produce; don't neglect them.
