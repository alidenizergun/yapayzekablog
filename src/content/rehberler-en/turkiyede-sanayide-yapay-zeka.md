There is no single support door for AI in Turkish industry. The Ministry of Industry and Technology, the Small and Medium Enterprises Development Organization (KOSGEB) and The Scientific and Technological Research Council of Turkey (TÜBİTAK) each step in with different tools. This guide summarizes the programs and figures that can be verified in sources, then explains how a business can get started and what it should watch for. Because eligibility rules and amounts can change, always check the relevant institution's official announcement before making a decision.

## Think of support in three layers

The support mentioned in sources can be grouped as follows: the ministry's digital transformation programs, KOSGEB's loan program dedicated to AI, and TÜBİTAK's call for the AI ecosystem. The first focuses on digitizing a company's production processes, the second on access to financing, and the third on turning AI technologies into products or solutions. Which one fits you depends on your company's size, the nature of your project and the data you have.

## Digital Transformation Program: the figures in the news

According to news reports, as of September 2026, 85 companies from the machinery, automotive, textile, chemicals, food and electronics sectors had applied to the ministry's Digital Transformation Program ([AA report, Foreks](https://www.foreks.com/haber/detay/6ae320d4422c786f3f7ac697/AA/tr/sanayide-dijital-ve-yesil-donusume-yapay-zeka-destegi-29-10-26/)). The same report mentions a figure of 21 billion lira. However, in another article from the same news series, this amount is described as investment rather than applications ([Haber7](https://ekonomi.haber7.com/ekonomi/haber/3672509-sanayide-yesil-ve-dijital-donusum-hamlesi-1655-milyar-liralik-yatirim-yolda)). For this reason, it is more reliable to read the figure as a sign of interest in the program rather than as a firm investment amount.

The same reports say the program will encourage the integration of AI, big data analytics and advanced automation into production processes, and will carry out sector-based analyses. The goal, in other words, is not just to buy software but to have solutions that work integrated into the production line.

## SME Digital Transformation Support Program

For small and medium-sized enterprises (KOBİs), the numbers are more concrete. Under the SME Digital Transformation Support Program, 3.5 billion lira in projects were approved from 284 applications, and 139 businesses used 1.4 billion lira in loans ([Ekonomi Politikası](https://ekonomipolitikasi.com.tr/sanayide-yapay-zeka-destekli-ikiz-donusum-hizlaniyor/)).

According to news reports, SMEs will receive digital maturity assessment consulting, and the European Digital Innovation Hubs will provide infrastructure, access to financing and training support ([Uzmanpara](https://uzmanpara.milliyet.com.tr/uzmanpara/sanayide-dijital-ve-yesil-donusume-yapay-zeka-destegi-7690011)). If you want to see where your business stands digitally before moving to AI, this type of maturity assessment may be a sensible first step. If you are looking for a general framework for smaller businesses, see our guide to [AI for small businesses](/rehber/kucuk-isletmeler-icin-yapay-zeka/).

## KOSGEB AI Loan Program

KOSGEB's AI Loan Program is offered through the GO Digital Wallet, with applications accepted from July 9 to December 31, 2026 ([Kadın Girişimcilerimizle Güçlü Yarınlara](https://kadingirisimci.gov.tr/duyurular/kosgeb-teknoloji-girisimlerine-yapay-zeka-kredi-programi/), [Bigpara](https://bigpara.hurriyet.com.tr/haberler/ekonomi-haberleri/yapay-zeka-girisimlerine-yeni-destek_ID102207130/)). Loan amounts range from 500,000 lira to 5 million lira, and news reports say no interest or commission is charged.

A practical warning: for details on who is eligible, which expenses are covered and the repayment terms, rely on KOSGEB's own announcement rather than aggregator sites or blog posts. It is also worth speaking with your certified public accountant before applying.

## AI on the green transformation side

AI is part of the green transformation agenda, not just efficiency. The total budget of the Turkey Green Industry Project is $450 million. As of September 2026, there were 186 active applications, 139 of them from large-scale companies and 47 from SMEs ([Uzmanpara](https://uzmanpara.milliyet.com.tr/uzmanpara/sanayide-dijital-ve-yesil-donusume-yapay-zeka-destegi-7690011)). News reports also say that developing the green transformation certification system with AI is a goal included in the 2027 Presidential Annual Program ([İstanbul Ticaret Gazetesi](https://istanbulticaretgazetesi.com/kobilere-yapay-zeka-ve-dijital-donusum-destegi-yeni-program-aciklandi)). This suggests that AI-supported data collection may become more important for exporters and companies that must report their carbon footprint. However, how this goal will be implemented is not yet a concrete obligation for any individual business.

## Where to read the strategy and TÜBİTAK calls

Read the goals of the National Artificial Intelligence Strategy related to industry from the document itself, not from commentary. The strategy text is [on TÜBİTAK BİLGEM's website](https://www.bilgem.tubitak.gov.tr/wp-content/uploads/sites/8/TR-UlusalYZStratejisi2021-2025-1.pdf), and the ministry's industrial policy framework is in the [2030 Industry and Technology Strategy](https://www.sanayi.gov.tr/assets/img/popup-image/pdf/SanayiTeknolojiStratejisi2030.pdf). Strategies provide useful background for understanding which themes support calls may prioritize.

TÜBİTAK has announced that applications for the 2026 round of the 1711 AI Ecosystem Call have opened. The aim of the call is to turn AI technologies into products or solutions that meet needs, and to activate Turkey's AI ecosystem ([TÜBİTAK's announcement on X](https://x.com/Tubitak/status/2067201562091340261)). For details on scope, eligibility and timelines, review the call text directly.

## How to get started

Following this sequence before applying for support will make your work easier:

- **Define the problem.** Instead of "we want to use AI," choose a measurable goal such as "reducing scrap rates" or "cutting unplanned downtime." For example areas, see our guides on [AI in manufacturing](/rehber/uretimde-yapay-zeka/), [predictive maintenance](/rehber/kestirimci-bakim-nedir/) and [image processing for quality control](/rehber/goruntu-isleme-ile-kalite-kontrol/).
- **Check your data.** Do you have sensor logs, maintenance histories or images of production defects, and are they kept in an organized way? If no data exists, the first project may be data collection.
- **Start with a small pilot.** Test on a single line or machine, measure the results, and only then consider scaling up.
- **Match the type of support.** Consulting, loans and R&D calls serve different needs; choose the one that fits your project's stage.
- **Write the rules.** Prepare an internal AI policy that specifies which data employees may enter into which tools.

## Risks and limits

Receiving support does not mean the project will succeed. Pay particular attention to three points. First, figures and percentages in news reports are sometimes reported inconsistently; as with the 21 billion lira figure above, the same number can appear with different meanings in different places. Second, support rates and upper limits listed on blogs and consulting sites should not form the basis of your application plan unless they are verified against an official text. Third, loan-based support carries a repayment obligation; being interest-free does not mean the project costs nothing.

This guide does not provide detailed examples of AI applications at industrial companies. For a broader overview of AI uses in the sector, see our page on [AI in Turkish industry](/rehber/turkiyede-sanayide-yapay-zeka/). In all cases, rely on a company's own statements and official announcements, and do not accept efficiency claims in marketing language without measuring them in your own processes.
