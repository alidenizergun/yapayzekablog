Artificial intelligence is not only bringing new tools; it is also changing job titles. Some roles are growing fast, some are being renamed, and some are known for precarious working conditions. This guide offers a balanced summary of research based on job posting data and the skills needed to move into these roles. Most of the figures come from the US market and secondary sources such as blogs, so read them as a directional guide rather than a firm conclusion.

## What the data says overall

According to [LinkedIn data published by the WEF](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2026/01/ai-has-already-added-1-3-million-new-jobs-according-to-linkedin-data/), AI has created 1.3 million new roles globally. These include AI engineers, forward-deployed engineers who work directly with customers, and data labelers.

According to [Dice's October 2026 report](https://www.dice.com/hiring/recruitment/reports/dice-tech-job-report), postings for AI and machine learning technology roles rose 91% year over year, while overall technology postings grew 20%. There is also a pay gap: in a [LinkedIn study reported by CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/make-it/2026/08/18/millennials-and-gen-z-are-landing-fast-growing-high-paying-ai-jobs-linkedin-study.html), the median salary for AI roles was about $177,000, compared with $80,000 for non-AI roles. The same report says that in 2025 only 26% of new hires in AI roles were women, compared with 50% in non-AI occupations. In other words, growth is not being distributed equally.

For a broader view of career and workforce change, you can also read our [AI and the workforce guide](/rehber/yapay-zeka-ve-is-gucu-meslekler-nasil-degisiyor/).

## AI engineer

This role connects models to products and business processes. In [LinkedIn's 2026 job postings](https://opendatascience.com/12-in-demand-ai-job-roles-2026-how-to-get-hired/), the most frequently mentioned technical skills include LangChain, RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) and PyTorch. In the same Dice report, postings that name AI coding tools such as Claude AI and GitHub Copilot grew by more than 200% year over year.

This shows the role is about much more than training models. In practice, it also involves running systems, maintaining them and auditing their output. For entry-level candidates, the numbers should be read more cautiously; more postings do not mean that entry-level jobs will be easy to find.

## Prompt engineer: job title or skill?

Prompt engineering was a widely discussed role in 2023, but it has since declined noticeably. According to data [attributed to Indeed Hiring Lab](https://www.responsibleaifoundation.com/post/why-prompt-engineering-was-always-temporary), searches for prompt engineer roles fell from 144 per million searches in April 2023 to roughly 20-30 in 2025. Because this data comes from a secondary source, the direction matters more than the exact figure.

The decline does not necessarily mean the skill has lost value. In Dice's data, prompt skills now appear mostly within AI engineer postings. Rather than searching for it as a standalone title, it may be more realistic to demonstrate the skill within your software, product or content work. To get started, see our guide on [how to write a good AI prompt](/rehber/iyi-istem-nasil-yazilir/).

## AI governance and ethics specialist

As companies use AI, questions about risk, compliance and accountability are growing, which is creating demand for governance and ethics roles. According to [analysis by Axial Search](https://axialsearch.com/insights/ai-governance-careers), 1,997 AI governance job postings appeared in the US between January and August 2026, an average of 71 new postings per week.

The profile of applicants is also notable. In the same analysis, the ratio of candidates with technical backgrounds to those with work-experience backgrounds was about 5-to-1, suggesting the role is read first as a technical function. The AIGP certification appears in 8.7% of postings, so it is not a requirement, but it is seen as a possible differentiator.

Knowledge of regulation also matters in this field. Our guide to an [internal AI policy for companies](/rehber/sirket-ici-yapay-zeka-politikasi/) is also useful for in-house implementation.

## Data labeling and AI training

Models continue to need human feedback and labeled data (training data). In Dice's report, the AI trainer title is among the titles that grew by more than 150% month over month. The LinkedIn data published by the WEF also counts data labelers among the new roles created by AI.

However, job security in this field can be weak. According to records about Sama, Meta reportedly ended its contract with Sama in March-April 2026, and more than 1,000 workers lost their jobs. The same source says that Time reported in 2023 that Kenyan workers employed for OpenAI were paid less than $2 an hour. This work may be an easy way in, but before applying, be sure to check pay, contract length and workload.

## The situation in Turkey

AI engineer postings are visible in Turkey. For example, Tosyalı Holding's [AI Engineer posting](https://www.techcareer.net/jobs/detail/yapay-zeka-muhendisi-4382280) dated February 17, 2026, is based in Osmaniye and covers MLOps and model retraining tasks. This shows that industrial companies are also looking for this role. For the industrial side, see our guide to [AI in Turkish industry](/rehber/turkiyede-sanayide-yapay-zeka/).

Most of the governance, prompt and data labeling figures in this article are based on US job postings. Numbers on job pages in Turkey are also snapshots, so do not read them as a permanent market measure. Scan current job listings yourself before applying.

## Skills for moving into these roles

In Dice's October 2026 data, the fastest-growing skills on a monthly basis include complex problem solving (72%) and AI applications (55%). The same data also highlights skills such as technical debt, maintenance and design review, which points to demand for engineers who can review AI-assisted code.

A practical roadmap could look like this:

- **For engineering:** Start with Python and PyTorch, then build a small project with tools such as RAG and LangChain. For a starting point, see our guide on [where to begin learning AI](/rehber/yapay-zeka-ogrenmeye-nereden-baslamali/).
- **For governance:** Bring your existing background in privacy, compliance, auditing or risk into an AI context. Because the data shows technical candidates are favored, do not neglect basic technical literacy.
- **For data labeling:** If you have subject-matter expertise (law, healthcare, languages), highlight it, but read pay and contract terms in advance.
- **For prompt skills:** Rather than searching for a separate title, produce measurable results in your current job and show them in your portfolio.

## Risks and limits

Many of the figures in this area come from blogs and aggregator sites, and LinkedIn's own report pages are often relayed through third parties. Growth rates for the same role can vary by source and period, so do not directly compare percentages from two different reports.

Salary figures are also US-based and may not apply to Turkey. Before changing your title, review the postings of the companies you are targeting, along with their expected experience and education requirements. Applying with exaggerated claims about your AI skills is easily exposed in an interview.
