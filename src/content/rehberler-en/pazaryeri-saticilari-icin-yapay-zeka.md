If you sell on a marketplace, AI is no longer just external tools like ChatGPT; platforms are also adding assistants and automations to seller panels. This guide covers the AI tools from Trendyol, Hepsiburada, Amazon and Shopify that can be confirmed through news reports, the rules to follow when creating content, and the risks of fake reviews and misleading visuals. A significant part of the platform information comes from news organizations' reporting rather than the companies' own announcement pages. Always verify in your own account whether a feature is active on your panel.

## Trendyol: the Ortak assistant and Trendyol LLM

According to Trendyol Group CEO Erdem İnan's remarks at Slush'D Istanbul, Trendyol LLM allows sellers to export in 10 languages, and an assistant called Ortak is helping sellers streamline their business processes ([DHA](https://www.dha.com.tr/ekonomi/trendyol-yapay-zeka-ile-e-ticaretin-gelecegini-sekillendiriyor-2963469)). As reported by Anadolu Agency, Trendyol has announced that its AI-driven data analysis gives sellers demand-based decision support for inventory management and product design ([Anadolu Ajansı](https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/isdunyasi/e-ticaret/trendyoldan-e-ticarette-yapay-zeka-odakli-donusum/705006)).

In practice, this offers two opportunities: preparing product descriptions in different languages, and basing inventory and demand decisions on data. The reports do not explain in detail which features are available to which sellers in the panel. Start by reviewing the assistant and tools sections in your panel, then test with a single product group.

## Hepsiburada: AI on the advertising side

On the Hepsiburada side, the most prominent seller-facing announcement concerns its advertising platform, HepsiAd. In an announcement dated October 16, 2026, the company said it had updated its HepsiAd solutions with new AI-focused technologies and self-service applications. The new applications are reported to help small and medium-sized businesses manage their campaign processes in-house ([CNBC-e](https://www.cnbce.com/sirket-haberleri/hepsiburadanin-reklam-platformunda-yapay-zeka-donemi-h39611)).

A sensible approach is to start with a small budget and track campaign results weekly. Beyond this, there is no confirmed announcement of a new AI assistant that Hepsiburada offers to sellers, so do not plan around the assumption that another tool exists.

## Amazon: Seller Assistant and Ads Data Manager

According to the Accelerate 2026 recap on the Amazon Selling Partners blog, Seller Assistant is a free, conversational AI tool available in the Seller Central panel. At the event, the tool was also extended to advertising, and a free tool called Ads Data Manager was introduced ([Amazon Selling Partners](https://sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com/accelerate-2026-recap)).

The same recap says the Selling Partner plugin lets sellers connect their Seller Central data to external AI tools such as Anthropic's Claude and Amazon Quick, and that this has so far started in the US. There is no clear information on whether these tools are available for accounts in Turkey, so check your own panel. Before connecting your data to an external tool, read what information will be shared; our [personal data protection guide](/rehber/yapay-zeka-kullanirken-kisisel-verileri-koruma/) can help with this.

## Shopify: the Sidekick assistant

Shopify's Sidekick assistant can generate product descriptions and marketing copy based on store data. According to Digital Commerce 360, the 2025 update also allows it to generate images ([Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/05/08/shopify-sidekick-ai-tool-ecommerce-platform-update/)). For Shopify, the information relies on news organizations' reporting; the scope of features and access by region may vary.

Sidekick's generative AI features work on the general principle we describe in our [AI use cases in e-commerce](/rehber/e-ticarette-yapay-zeka-kullanim-alanlari/) guide: a good output depends on a good input and your oversight. It produces drafts quickly, but you make the final decision.

## Fake reviews regulation and AI

According to the Ministry of Trade, an amendment to the Regulation on Commercial Advertising and Unfair Commercial Practices, published in Official Gazette No. 33297 dated July 1, 2026, took effect on August 1, 2026 ([Haberler.com](https://www.haberler.com/ekonomi/ticaret-bakanligi-sahte-urun-yorumlarina-yonelik-20286019-haberi/)). Under the regulation, only people who have purchased the product can leave reviews, and reviews are published without distinguishing between positive and negative ones and remain online for at least one year. Sellers that fail to comply are also reported to face administrative sanctions ([Öğün Haber](https://www.ogunhaber.com/genel/internette-sahte-yorum-donemi-bitiyor-hizmeti-satin-almayan-kisiler-yorum-yapamayacak-3002450h.html)).

Within this framework, generating or having reviews written in the name of people who have not bought the product conflicts with the regulation, even if AI is used. Use AI to summarize incoming reviews and improve your product and service, not to write reviews. Read the Official Gazette text yourself, and consult a lawyer for a legal assessment of your own situation.

## Misleading visuals and content risk

A blog post notes that AI can be used for tasks such as background removal and light and color correction, but that an image altering product features may be considered misleading. According to the same post, a product that lacks the stated features may be considered defective goods under Law No. 6502 on Consumer Protection ([Webaction](https://webaction.com.tr/yapay-zeka-kullanarak-urun-fotograflari-duzenlenebilir-mi/)). Since this is a blog report, verify the legal provision from an official source.

There is no verified information that Turkey's marketplaces have banned the use of AI, but each marketplace has its own main image and content rules. Read these rules in the seller panel guidelines. Practical principles include:

- Do not change the color, size, texture or accessories of the product in an AI-generated image.
- The image on the product page and in the ad should show the same product and the same variant.
- Do not replace a real photo with an AI image; make edits on the real photo. For details, see our [AI product photography](/rehber/yapay-zeka-ile-urun-fotografi/) guide.

There is a similar risk with text: large language models can generate made-up information (hallucinations). Verify dimensions, materials, compliance and warranty details manually.

## How to get started

1. Review your panel: check whether the assistants on Trendyol, Amazon and Shopify are active in your account.
2. Start with a single task, such as the descriptions for one product group or one advertising campaign.
3. Before publishing any output, use a short checklist that compares it with the product's actual features.
4. Protect your brand voice; our guide to [AI content creation and brand voice](/rehber/yapay-zeka-ile-icerik-uretimi-ve-marka-sesi/) can help.
5. Measure results: track whether campaign performance, return reasons and customer complaints improve.

AI tools can speed up a seller's work, but they do not remove responsibility for marketplace rules and consumer law. While gaining speed, do not compromise on the accuracy of your content.
