AI tools that generate images from text can turn a few sentences of description into an image in seconds. The quality of the result depends largely on how well that description is written. The method below works with most image generation tools.

## 1. Describe the subject clearly

State the main element of the image and what it is doing: instead of "A cat," write "An elderly orange-furred cat sunbathing by a window." Details such as number, color, age, material and emotion reduce the amount the model has to guess.

## 2. Add the setting and time

Specify where and when the scene takes place: "In an old Istanbul apartment building, early in the morning, with soft light coming in through the window." The setting is one of the most important factors shaping an image's atmosphere.

## 3. Define the style

Is it a photograph, an illustration, a watercolor, a 3D rendering? Phrases like "realistic photo, 35 mm lens," "flat-color minimal illustration" or "children's book illustration" change the result a great deal. Describing the characteristics of a style, rather than using the name of a living artist as the style, is both more ethical and easier to control.

## 4. Specify framing, lighting and color palette

Framing descriptions such as "close-up," "bird's-eye view," "wide-angle landscape" and "blurred background"; lighting descriptions such as "golden hour light," "studio lighting" and "dim and cinematic"; and palette choices such as "earth tones" and "pastel colors" make an image look more professional.

## 5. State the aspect ratio and intended use

Think about where you will use the image: a square ratio suits an Instagram post, vertical suits a story, and horizontal suits a website header. Most tools offer an aspect ratio option or let you specify it in the prompt. If you plan to add text over the image, include instructions such as "leave empty space on the left for text."

## 6. Generate variations and refine step by step

Pick the best of the first results and describe only what you want changed: "The same image, but make the cat asleep" or "Reduce the objects in the background." Some tools also let you edit only a section of the image that you select.

## 7. Follow copyright and ethics rules

- Don't produce fake images of real people, especially well-known ones; this may fall under deepfake rules and can lead to legal trouble.
- Avoid images that copy brand logos and well-known cartoon characters.
- For images that could be perceived as news, documents or evidence, disclose that they were generated by AI.

## Example prompt

"Hot air balloons floating in the sky at sunrise in Cappadocia, fairy chimneys in the foreground, realistic photo, wide angle, warm and soft light, light haze, horizontal 16:9 ratio, plain sky area at the top for a headline."

Because this prompt provides the subject, setting, style, framing, lighting, aspect ratio and intended use together, it has a high chance of producing a usable result on the first try.
