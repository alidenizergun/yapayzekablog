Finding someone to run a mock interview with you is not always easy. AI assistants can play the interviewer and ask you questions even at midnight, then give feedback on your answers. Used well, they are a powerful preparation tool that builds confidence.

## 1. Have the AI analyze the job posting and your résumé together

Give the AI the job posting and your résumé, then ask: "List the 15 most likely interview questions for this position, grouped as behavioral, technical and role-specific. Also point out the parts of my résumé the interviewer might press on." This list becomes the backbone of your preparation.

## 2. Prepare your stories with the STAR method

The best answer to behavioral questions ("How did you handle a difficult customer?") is a concrete story. The STAR method walks through the situation (Situation), your task (Task), what you did (Action) and the outcome (Result), in that order. Write up your own experience roughly, then say: "Rework this into the STAR structure, at a length that can be told in 1.5 minutes; do not add made-up details."

## 3. Run a mock interview

**Prompt template:** "You are an experienced hiring manager interviewing for a [position] role at a [type of company] company. Ask me only one question at a time, wait for my answer, then give brief feedback and move on to the next question. Ask 8 questions in total and give an overall assessment at the end."

Practicing out loud with the voice conversation feature in mobile apps gives you an experience closer to a real interview.

## 4. Make the feedback concrete

Instead of general comments like "That was good," ask for an evaluation against criteria: "Score my answer from 1 to 5 on clarity, concrete examples, emphasis on results and length, and tell me how to improve it." Then try the same question again with your revised answer.

## 5. Research the company

With an assistant that can search the web, you can ask about the company's products, recent news and position in its industry. Open the sources it shows and check them; models can mix up company information. Also prepare 3-4 thoughtful questions to ask at the end of the interview.

## 6. Prepare for the salary discussion

AI can help you rehearse the phrases you will use in the salary discussion and your responses to likely objections. However, for salary ranges, do not rely on the model's guess; rely on current salary surveys, industry reports and what people you know can tell you.

## 7. Write a thank-you email after the interview

A short thank-you email after the interview leaves a good impression. Have the assistant draft a 4-5 sentence note that refers to a topic discussed in the meeting, then edit it to match your own style.
