The hardest part of learning a foreign language is finding someone to practice with regularly. AI assistants can offer speaking, writing and reading practice at any hour, patiently and without judgment. The methods below work for most languages, especially English.

## 1. State your level and goal

Tell the assistant your level (A1-C2, or beginner, intermediate, advanced) and why you are learning: business meetings, travel, an exam, academic reading. An opening like "I'm at B1 level and want to express myself more comfortably in business meetings" shapes all the exercises that follow.

## 2. Practice speaking

In apps with a voice conversation feature, chat with the assistant out loud. **Prompt template:** "Speak English with me. You are a hotel receptionist, and I am a guest checking in. Keep your sentences short, and briefly correct my mistakes after each of my answers." Role play lets you prepare for real situations such as interviews, restaurants, doctor's appointments or client meetings.

## 3. Ask for reading texts that match your level

Have it write short texts at your level on a topic you care about: "Write a 200-word text about football at B1 level, and below it add the Turkish meanings of difficult words and 5 comprehension questions." Topics you find interesting make learning easier.

## 4. Work on vocabulary

Give it the new words you have learned and ask for example sentences, synonyms and differences in usage. You can review by saying, "Prepare a different short quiz for me every day with these 10 words." It also helps to transfer the lists the assistant prepares into a flashcard app.

## 5. Have your writing corrected

Give it a paragraph you wrote and ask not only for the corrected version but also for an explanation of the mistakes: "Correct the mistakes in this text, explain the reason for each correction in Turkish, and give me a similar exercise." That way you learn not to repeat the same mistake.

## 6. Learn grammar through examples

Ask about a rule you don't understand: "Explain the difference between the present perfect and the past simple with 5 examples from daily life, then give me a 10-question exercise." The assistant's explanations are a good starting point; for critical topics, compare them with a grammar reference.

## 7. Build a regular routine

Short daily sessions of 15-20 minutes are more effective than long sessions once a week. You can ask the assistant to prepare a weekly study plan. Create opportunities to talk with real people whenever possible; AI is a tool for preparing for those opportunities.
