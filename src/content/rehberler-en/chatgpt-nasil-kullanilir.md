ChatGPT is an AI assistant developed by OpenAI that you can ask questions by typing (or speaking). Anthropic's Claude and Google's Gemini work on the same principle, and the steps in this guide apply to all three. The location and names of menus may change over time, but the basic usage stays the same.

## 1. Create an account and choose your app

You can use the assistant in a web browser or through a mobile app. Download the app only from an official app store or the company's own website; look-alike fake apps with similar names can be found in app stores. Once you have an account, the message box at the bottom of the screen is where you talk to the assistant.

## 2. Write your first question clearly and in detail

Don't talk to the assistant as if you were typing into a search engine; talk to it as if you were briefing a colleague. Writing "Suggest three cities for a 4-day trip in March, no more than 2 hours by plane from Istanbul, with museums and a mid-range budget" instead of "vacation suggestions" will produce a far more useful answer. State what you want, who it is for and in what format you want it (list, table, short paragraph).

## 3. Refine the answer within the conversation

If the first answer isn't what you expected, ask for changes in the same conversation instead of opening a new one: "Make it shorter," "Explain the second point," "Turn this into a table," "Use a more formal tone." Because the assistant remembers earlier messages, you don't have to explain everything from scratch each time. When the topic changes completely, however, opening a new conversation gives cleaner results.

## 4. Try file, photo and voice features

Most current assistants are multimodal: you can upload a PDF, Word or Excel file and have it summarized, have it read the text in a photo, or ask it to interpret a chart. In phone apps, you can also work with the assistant by talking to it using the voice feature. Before uploading a file, check whether it contains personal or confidential information.

## 5. Watch out for web search and up-to-date information

Models know information only up to the date their training data was collected. For questions such as current news, prices or match results, check whether the assistant has run a web search and which sources it cites. Treat up-to-date information that comes without sources with caution.

## 6. Check the answers

AI assistants write fluently and confidently, but they can sometimes make up information that doesn't exist (hallucination). Verify figures, dates, names, quotations and advice on topics such as health, law and finance with reliable sources. Asking the assistant, "What is the source of this information?" can also help.

## 7. Review your privacy settings

The settings section includes options such as whether your conversation history is stored and whether your conversations are used to train the model. Do not type ID numbers, passwords, card details or other people's personal data into the assistant. For details, see our [guide to protecting personal data](/rehber/yapay-zeka-kullanirken-kisisel-verileri-koruma/).

## 5 things you can try on day one

- **Email draft:** "Write a short, polite email to a customer explaining that the delivery date will be postponed by a week."
- **Summary:** Paste in a long article and say, "Summarize this in 5 bullet points."
- **Explanation:** "Explain the relationship between inflation and interest rates as if to a high school student."
- **Plan:** "Prepare a 30-minute walking program for 3 days a week."
- **Ideas:** "Give me 10 promotion ideas for my coffee shop."
