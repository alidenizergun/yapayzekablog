The hard part of a good presentation is less the slide design than deciding what to say and in what order. AI assistants speed up that thinking process, simplify slide text and can play the audience during rehearsal.

## 1. Define the purpose and the audience

**Prompt template:** "I'm preparing a [length]-minute presentation for [audience]. My goal is to [get a decision / inform / persuade]. My main message is: … The information I have is: … Suggest an 8-10 slide flow; for each slide, write a title and a one-sentence key message."

A board presentation and a training seminar are structured very differently, so stating the audience and goal changes the flow completely.

## 2. Challenge the story flow

Ask the assistant to critique the proposed flow: "Is there a place in this flow that weakens its persuasiveness? What objections will the audience raise, and on which slide should each be answered?" The sequence of problem, solution, evidence and call to action is a good starting point for most business presentations.

## 3. Simplify the slide text

Slides are not documents to be read; they are visuals that support your talk. An instruction such as "Use at most 3 bullet points per slide, each no longer than 8 words; move the details to the speaker notes" prevents crowded slides. Have titles written as messages, not topics: instead of "Sales figures," use "Sales have grown for three quarters."

## 4. Get visual and chart suggestions

Give the assistant your data and ask, "Which chart type is best for showing this data?" A bar chart for comparisons and a line chart for change over time are often the clearest choices. You can also create cover or section visuals with tools that can generate images; our [image creation guide](/rehber/yapay-zeka-ile-gorsel-olusturma/) can help with this.

## 5. Prepare speaker notes

Have the assistant write out, point by point, what you will say for each slide, then adapt it to your own way of speaking. Rather than memorizing the notes, keep them short so they simply remind you of the main points.

## 6. Rehearse and prepare for questions

You can prepare for the Q&A with a prompt like "You are a skeptical executive listening to this presentation; ask me the 5 hardest questions." You can also give it your presentation text and have it estimate the running time: in Turkish, people are assumed to speak an average of 120-150 words per minute.

## 7. Final check

Make sure the figures, dates and sources are correct. Do not put statistics suggested by AI into your presentation without finding their source. Check that the slides follow your brand guidelines and use a readable font size.
