The most talked-about topic this week was Nvidia. U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent asked the company's board to respond within 10 days about its Chinese chip sales and said the next CEO should be chosen from outside the company. The earnings report that followed showed revenue up 103%, but the board announced that Jensen Huang, who resigned, will not return.  OpenAI also made GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol models available.

There was also a busy week in business and policy. Palo Alto Networks agreed to buy Applied Compute for $4.5 billion. The Trump administration was reported to be planning to designate more than 24 AI companies as "national champions." In Turkey, the Ministry of Justice began preparing a regulation targeting fake images and voices produced with AI. At the end of the week, the selloff in AI and chip stocks pushed U.S. indexes into correction territory.
