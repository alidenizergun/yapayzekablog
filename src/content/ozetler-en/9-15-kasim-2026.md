The week's biggest development came at Nvidia, the leader of the AI chip market: founder and CEO Jensen Huang resigned, and allegations about him also put chip export controls back on the agenda in Washington. Heavy demand for Anthropic's IPO, meanwhile, showed that investor interest in AI companies continues.

On the product side, AI agents were the main theme. OpenAI and Meta launched new features in which AI does more than answer questions: it shops and makes phone calls on the user's behalf.
