The UK's AI Security Institute (UK AISI) developed a universal jailbreak (a prompt that bypasses safety measures) against two preview versions of OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra model. The effort required half as many queries as it took against GPT-6 Astra.

OpenAI's system card, published on November 17, also indicates a noticeable decline in robustness compared with GPT-6 Astra. The company further says the model has crossed its own "Critical" threshold for cybersecurity capabilities.

Xander Davies, red team lead at UK AISI, wrote on X that the jailbreak was developed by a single researcher.
