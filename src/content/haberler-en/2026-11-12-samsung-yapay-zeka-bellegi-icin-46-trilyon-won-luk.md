The first groundbreaking under South Korea's "megaproject" program for major industrial investments took place Thursday at Samsung's advanced chip packaging facility. President Lee Jae Myung said a 6 trillion won advanced packaging plant will be built in Onyang, followed by a phased 40 trillion won investment in advanced equipment. Total investment comes to 46 trillion won, or about $34 billion.

Samsung has moved up the facility's start of operations by one year, to June 2028. The company plans to begin mass production of high-bandwidth memory (HBM), used in AI servers, in May 2029.

The investment program was announced in July; what is new is the groundbreaking ceremony and the earlier timeline. An industry source speaking to AJP said it is hard to predict at this stage how far the schedule can be accelerated.
