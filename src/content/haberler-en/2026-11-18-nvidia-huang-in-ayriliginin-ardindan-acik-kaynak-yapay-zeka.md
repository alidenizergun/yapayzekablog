Nvidia's interim CEO Colette Kress reaffirmed the company's open-source AI commitment on Tuesday during an earnings call with analysts. The call was the first earnings call held since CEO Jensen Huang's resignation.

Kress's remarks indicated that plans for Nvidia's acquisition of Hugging Face have not changed. The platform, which serves more than 118 million developers and 200,000 companies, is still planned to join Nvidia. The acquisition was not addressed in the call's Q&A session.

Kress also said Nvidia's Nemotron family of open-weight models is the most widely used U.S.-developed open model on OpenRouter.

The report is based on information relayed by TechCrunch; it could not be checked against the official page of Nvidia's earnings announcement.
