Microsoft announced the AI Transparency Initiative at its Ignite conference. Satya Nadella made the announcement. More than 40 organizations, including Accenture, Adobe, KPMG, Mastercard and Volvo Cars, have joined the initiative.

The initiative aims to establish shared standards for security and governance across models and providers.

Microsoft will also expand its participation in the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology's (NIST) AI Agent Standards Initiative.
