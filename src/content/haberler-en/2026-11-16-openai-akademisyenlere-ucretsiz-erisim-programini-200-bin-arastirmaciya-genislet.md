OpenAI has expanded ChatGPT for Academic Researchers to reach 200,000 researchers. The 50,000 new grants opening on November 23 will give each grant recipient and up to two collaborators Plus access.

The company says 20,000 researchers from 33 countries have joined since the program launched in July. OpenAI also reported that in a study of more than 1,800 participants, scientists' productivity nearly doubled.

Data shared within the program will not be used for model training by default.
