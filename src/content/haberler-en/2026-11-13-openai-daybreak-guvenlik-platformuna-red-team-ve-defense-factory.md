OpenAI has added two new products to its Daybreak security platform. Daybreak Red Team consists of a group of AI agents that work together to run penetration tests on web applications. Daybreak Defense Factory focuses on fixing the security vulnerabilities that are found.

Red Team is available in early access. Defense Factory is opening as a preview in eligible ChatGPT workspaces. The products were introduced during the "Cyber at the Frontier" broadcast on November 12. OpenAI President Greg Brockman also announced the products the same day on his X account.

According to The Register, the agents currently use OpenAI's GPT-6 Cyber model. Fixes proposed by the agents are reviewed and approved by engineers before being applied to live systems. Pricing has not yet been announced.
