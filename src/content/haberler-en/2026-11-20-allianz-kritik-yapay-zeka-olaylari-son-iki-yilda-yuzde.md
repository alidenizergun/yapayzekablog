Allianz Commercial has published a report showing that AI is creating new risks for businesses. According to the report, critical AI incidents have risen by more than 150% over the past two years.

The report states that in some cases, insurance sector losses could reach around $100 million.

The report reportedly warns that AI losses verified to date reflect only a small portion of the actual risk.
