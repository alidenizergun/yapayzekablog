LG AI Research won an award on November 4, 2026, at the Copyright Technology Awards held as part of the ICOTEC 2026 conference. The award was given for EXAONE Nexus, an AI agent the company developed for compliance processes.

The company reportedly received the prize as a presidential award, given on behalf of the head of South Korea's National Intellectual Property Commission.
