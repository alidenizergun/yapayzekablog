Physical AI research and deployment startup Humyn Labs said it has closed a $47 million Series A round. Accel led the $26 million equity portion of the round. The remaining $21 million came as venture debt led by Lighthouse Canton.

The company will use the new capital to build distribution infrastructure in Brazil, South Korea and India and to develop data collection hardware. Humyn Labs also plans to strengthen its presence in San Francisco, London and Seoul.

Humyn Labs claims it has reached more than $50 million in annualized revenue (annualized revenue) within the six months since it began operating.
