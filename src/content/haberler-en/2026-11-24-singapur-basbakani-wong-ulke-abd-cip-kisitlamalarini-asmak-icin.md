Singapore Prime Minister Lawrence Wong said his country does not want to be used as a pathway for companies seeking to get around US export controls. According to Bloomberg, Singaporean authorities are examining how Singapore-based Megaspeed bought Nvidia chips.

Speaking during a visit to Australia, Wong said Singapore does not enforce US export controls, but also does not want the country to be "misused" to circumvent them. He said Singapore would make it easier for US oversight and would impose "very firm" penalties on companies that violate local laws.

The report does not say which agency is conducting the review or whether Megaspeed has responded.
