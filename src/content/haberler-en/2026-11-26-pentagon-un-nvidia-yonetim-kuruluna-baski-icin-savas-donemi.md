In a report citing several people familiar with the matter, The Washington Post said Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth and other officials discussed using the Defense Production Act as leverage to force Nvidia's board to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO. The report says Deputy Defense Secretary Steve Feinberg also took part in the discussions. The talks reportedly took place the weekend before Huang returned as CEO on Monday.

The law, enacted in 1950, has traditionally been used to support military production during wartime or national emergencies. According to the report, the law does not provide a clear path for appointing a CEO. A Trump administration official said the law could be used to shift more of Nvidia's production toward military projects. A person close to the board said the board did not see this as a threat.

A senior Pentagon official told the Post that the described discussions never took place and that the defense secretary, his deputy or an undersecretary did not make such a proposal. It is unclear whether Trump or Vice President Vance was aware of the discussions.
