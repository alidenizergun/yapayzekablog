The California Government Operations Agency published its "AI Safety in 2026 and Beyond" report on November 18, after preparing it on November 16. The report recommends that major frontier AI developers meet state-defined safety standards.

Under the proposals, these companies would accept continuous oversight by licensed independent auditors and would be subject to emergency shutdown orders. The report also recommends setting aside CalCompute capacity for safety research.

Governor Gavin Newsom called on Congress and President Trump to adopt this framework or use it as a national baseline. State Sen. Jerry McNerney, meanwhile, announced he will draft a bill to implement the recommendations in the report.
