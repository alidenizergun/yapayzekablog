OpenAI says it has started "embedded assessment" work with Apollo Research, METR, Transluce and Goodfire. The outside experts will get deep access to the company's internal models and systems, with the aim of reviewing its alignment work and testing its safety safeguards.

Apollo will examine whether models can covertly act against the intentions of their developers, and whether safeguards can detect this. METR will investigate misaligned behavior in what the company describes as its largest reinforcement learning (RL) effort to date.

Transluce will review the company's latest post-training alignment evaluations and plans to expand the pilot in 2027.
