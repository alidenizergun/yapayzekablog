Google announced Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12, 2026. The company positions Carbon and Argon for tasks that require deep reasoning, while describing Hydrogen as the speed-focused model. Sundar Pichai and Logan Kilpatrick also announced the launch on their X accounts.

The model is available across all tiers of Google AI Studio, in the Gemini API, in Gemini for business, in the Gemini app and in Google Search. Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can use the model in Antigravity. Free Gemini users will be able to use Hydrogen when the app's automatic model selection kicks in.

According to Google, the model completes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash. It scores 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1, compared with 18.2% for Flash. Google also says the model's hallucination rate has fallen to under one-fifth. On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, the model reportedly scored 51 points at its default setting, while Argon scored 50.

## Pricing and technical details

API pricing is $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens. There are four thinking levels: low, medium, high (the default) and the new xhigh. Input, thinking and output share a single context window of 1,048,576 tokens in total. Google also announced an Orchestrator feature in Antigravity that switches between Hydrogen and Gemini 4 Carbon depending on the task.

An independent test reported by The Decoder, however, showed that Hydrogen rarely hallucinates but lags behind its competitors on terminal tasks.
