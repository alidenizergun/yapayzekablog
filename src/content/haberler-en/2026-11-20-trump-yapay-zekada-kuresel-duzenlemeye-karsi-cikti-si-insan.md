U.S. President Donald Trump spoke at the APEC meeting in Shenzhen, ahead of his meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping. Trump said he opposes any “global regulation” of artificial intelligence.

Xi, for his part, said the U.S. and China should cooperate to keep AI “under human control.” The two leaders’ approaches on this issue diverge.

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney also said on Wednesday that the lack of global AI governance poses a “tremendous risk.”
