Zoho has unveiled new AI features for support workflows as part of the 10th anniversary of its customer support software, Zoho Desk. The announcement was made on November 17, 2026.

Blended Agents, developed on the Zia Agents platform, is available starting with the Express plan of Zoho Desk. This means AI agents are offered not only in the top-tier plans but also in lower-tier plans.

The platform is used in 155 countries, including Türkiye. However, Blended Agents currently supports only English.
