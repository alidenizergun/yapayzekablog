On November 12, Anthropic announced its IPO page at ipo.anthropic.com with a post titled "Our initial public offering" on its official X account. The post also includes the legal disclaimer required by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

The price range had been disclosed earlier. According to Anthropic's updated filing with the SEC dated November 10, the company will offer 825 million Class A shares at $70-78 per share. The sale is expected to raise between $57.75 billion and $64.35 billion. This range corresponds to a valuation of up to $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis. The shares will trade on Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT."

On November 11, Anthropic also filed an amended securities registration statement with the Kanto Local Finance Bureau in Japan for the IPO.

## Timeline

According to CNBC, pricing is expected on November 18, with shares set to begin trading on November 19. The company has not yet officially announced these dates.
