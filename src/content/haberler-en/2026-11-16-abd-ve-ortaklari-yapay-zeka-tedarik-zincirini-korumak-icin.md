The US and its partners signed the San Francisco Declaration under the US-led Pax Silica initiative. The Financial Times reported it on November 15, citing Jacob Helberg, the State Department's Under Secretary for Economic Affairs.

Signatories agreed to create a joint list of critical AI technologies and supply chains to be protected. The text envisions working groups for weak points in the AI supply chain and "economic hubs" that would attract investment. The scope covers semiconductors, critical minerals and energy infrastructure.

The declaration was signed three days before the APEC leaders' meeting. Reports interpret the declaration as targeting China.
