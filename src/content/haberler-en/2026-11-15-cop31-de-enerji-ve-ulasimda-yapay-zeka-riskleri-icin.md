According to a Hürriyet report dated November 15, 2026, the risks of using AI in cross-border energy and transport systems were raised at COP31. Governance, infrastructure resilience and alignment problems between countries' legal frameworks stood out in the talks.

According to the warning cited in the report, the absence of strong governance and oversight mechanisms could leave digital systems in energy and transport infrastructure exposed to risks. Technical failures in these systems could reportedly lead to widespread power outages or disruptions to urban transport.

The Economist also covered the topic the same day under the heading "digital resilience."
