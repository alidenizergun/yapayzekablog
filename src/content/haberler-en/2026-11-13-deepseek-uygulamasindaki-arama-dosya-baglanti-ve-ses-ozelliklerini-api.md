DeepSeek has begun offering the app capabilities of its V4.1-Flash (deepseek-flash) model through the Messages, Responses and Chat Completions interfaces. Under the change, announced on November 13, tool calls and context preparation are handled on DeepSeek's servers, so developers no longer need to build these layers themselves.

The features include web and image search with source citations, deep search during multi-step reasoning, and the ability to attach PDFs, Office files and images to a chat. Link reading and safety checks are also part of the package. Search, page reading (Fetch), optical character recognition (OCR), speech-to-text (ASR) and text-to-speech (TTS) are also offered as standalone APIs.

According to DeepSeek's post on X, the company has also released an open-source front-end demo that supports importing DeepSeek conversation history.

## Pricing

According to DeepSeek's post on X, the expanded capabilities carry a tool usage fee on top of the standard token price. Another report, however, said the model's base token prices have not changed.
