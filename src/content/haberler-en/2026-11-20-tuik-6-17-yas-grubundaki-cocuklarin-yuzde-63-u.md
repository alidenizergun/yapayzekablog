According to TÜİK's survey, 80.1% of children aged 6-17 in Turkey use a mobile phone or smartphone. Of these children, 63% use AI. In other words, 8 out of every 10 children use a mobile phone or smartphone, and 6 out of every 10 use AI.

The survey addressed generative AI use for the first time this year. As a result, children's relationship with AI has become visible in official statistics through measurable data.

The data could serve as a reference in debates on education and digital safety.
