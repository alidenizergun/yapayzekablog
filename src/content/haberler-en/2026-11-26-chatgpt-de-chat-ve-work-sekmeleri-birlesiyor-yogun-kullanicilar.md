OpenAI has begun offering the combined Chat and Work experience in ChatGPT to a small group of Pro subscribers. Product lead Thibault Sottiaux said in a post on X that the rollout started on Tuesday. Speaking to TechCrunch, an OpenAI spokesperson said most users would not see their limits reset more often, but a small group of the heaviest users may reach their limits earlier.

Under the new system, Chat and Work share a single usage meter across ChatGPT. Conversations with the Instant model remain unlimited and do not count against the shared quota. Some subscribers, who continue to pay the same fee, are worried about losing access to advanced reasoning models.

According to OpenAI's help page, shared usage limits will reach a broader audience in December. The source does not say which plans will be affected.
