In reports dated November 21, 2026, a sharp selloff in US AI and chip stocks pushed the major indexes into correction territory. The Nasdaq fell 1,000 points, or 5.28%, while the Dow Jones dropped 1.93%. The S&P 500 lost 3.7%, falling to 6,910.39.

The Dow Jones and S&P 500 fell more than 10% below their record closing levels, entering correction territory. According to reports, the decline is driven by concerns over whether AI investments will deliver the expected gains, as well as interest rate expectations in the US.

AI and chip stocks, including Anthropic, Nvidia and AMD, also suffered steep losses.
