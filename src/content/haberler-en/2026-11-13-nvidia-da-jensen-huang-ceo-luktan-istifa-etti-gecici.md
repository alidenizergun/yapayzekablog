Nvidia announced on Thursday that Jensen Huang has resigned as president and CEO. According to a filing the company made with regulators, the board accepted the resignation on Wednesday. Huang thus ends his 33-year tenure as CEO.

CFO Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO and will continue as CFO until a permanent successor is found. The board is considering internal and external candidates for the permanent CEO role. Harvey Jones was reportedly named chair of Nvidia's board; Huang had held the chair and CEO roles together. Nvidia will report its financial results on Tuesday without a permanent CEO.

Nvidia did not give a reason for the resignation. The press, however, links the resignation to allegations about Huang. TechCrunch writes that Huang acknowledged a relationship with an alleged Chinese spy a few days ago. Nvidia shares fell more than 13% in morning trading, then recovered some of the losses.

## The Hugging Face acquisition

Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue wrote on X that the company will continue working for open-source AI. TechCrunch reports the post alongside questions about the fate of Nvidia's $12.9 billion acquisition of Hugging Face. The acquisition is scheduled to close in the first half of 2027.
