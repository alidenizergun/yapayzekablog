According to Semafor, both House Speaker Mike Johnson and Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries support tighter export controls on AI chips after Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's alleged ties to a purported Chinese spy came to light. Johnson said a consensus on the issue is beginning to form in Congress.

The Senate's draft defense authorization bill (NDAA) already includes the Chip Security Act, the AI Overwatch Act and the MATCH Act on AI chips. Semafor writes that the measure could become law by the end of the year. There is not yet an agreement on the final content.
