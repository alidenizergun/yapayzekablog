ByteDance and Alibaba have sought U.S. approval to ship more than 1.1 million Nvidia AI chips to data centers in Malaysia, Indonesia and Thailand. According to export license records published on November 19 by the U.S. House Foreign Affairs Committee, none of these requests have been approved.

The records show that U.S. export controls are directly restricting Chinese tech companies' data center plans in Southeast Asia.
