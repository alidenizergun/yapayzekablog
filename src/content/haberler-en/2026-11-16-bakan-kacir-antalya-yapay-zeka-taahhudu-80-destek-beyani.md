Industry and Technology Minister Mehmet Fatih Kacır said the Antalya AI Pledge, launched with Turkey's COP31 Presidency and the International Telecommunication Union (ITU), had received 80 statements of support and 271 concrete commitments as of November 16. Kacır announced the figures at the High-Level Green Digital Action Roundtable.

The pledge aims to have countries, companies and international organizations make voluntary commitments to use artificial intelligence responsibly in the fight against climate change.

Kacır said the goal is to expand the use of AI in areas such as industry, energy, agriculture and disaster management, raising efficiency and quality by at least 10%. He also noted that electricity consumption by AI-focused data centers rose 50% in 2025.
