Picsart has opened the AI models that run behind its platform to developers and businesses via an API (application programming interface). The announcement was made on November 20, 2026; the catalog reportedly covers more than 250 creative AI models.

New users can access the video model Wan 3.0 unlimited between November 20 and 22. Per-generation fees, rate limits and the full model list are not clear from the initial announcement.

Access to multiple creative models through a single API could make model selection and integration easier for app developers.
