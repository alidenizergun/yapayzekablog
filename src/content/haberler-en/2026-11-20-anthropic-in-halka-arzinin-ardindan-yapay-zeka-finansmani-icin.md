Following Anthropic's IPO, some Democratic politicians have renewed calls for new regulation in the AI sector. The report says Anthropic opened its first day of trading with a market value of $1.8 trillion.

Democratic Sen. Mark Warner introduced a bill on Thursday that would require publicly traded companies to disclose AI-linked financing.

A Democratic representative, meanwhile, said he is concerned that Anthropic is continuing its IPO despite outages affecting many critical systems.
