According to Reuters, the US and Russia appear set to oppose an effort at the UN meeting in Geneva to launch negotiations on legally binding rules for autonomous weapons. Most of the 128 states party to the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW) support binding rules. The US, Russia and Israel argue that existing international humanitarian law is sufficient.

The US is proposing a five-year negotiating mandate for a non-binding agreement. The meeting chair, meanwhile, proposed a two-year mandate for a binding protocol.

In a video message at the opening of the meeting, the UN Secretary-General urged states to move toward negotiating a binding instrument.
