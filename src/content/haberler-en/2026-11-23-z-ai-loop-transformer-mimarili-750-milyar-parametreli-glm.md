Z.ai (Zhipu AI) released its first flagship Loop Transformer model, GLM-5.4, on November 23. The model runs the 28 layers of the GLM-5 base model through twice in a loop, deepening the network without adding parameters. Vision support has also been added to the 750-billion-parameter model.

GLM-5.4 is available to all GLM Coding Plan users, as well as to ZCode and Z.ai Chat. API access will be released after a safety evaluation, and open weights will be published once safety improvements are complete; no specific date has been given.

Z.ai says the model reaches an intelligence level comparable to models with 2T-3T (2-3 trillion) parameters. This is the company's own claim; benchmark results for GLM-5.4 have not yet been shared. The company also acknowledges that the model occasionally falls into excessively long reasoning loops.
