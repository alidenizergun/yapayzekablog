Alibaba has officially announced Qwen4-Max, its new flagship model, following last week's preview release. The company disclosed the model's architecture, evaluation results and API pricing. The preview was made available on November 11.

The model uses a Mixture-of-Experts architecture with 2.4 trillion total parameters, native visual understanding and a 1 million-token context window. API pricing is $1.5 per million input tokens and $4.5 per million output tokens.

Alibaba says the model outperformed Qwen3.8-Max on its own Work360 test. According to the company, the average cost per task fell from $4.43 to $1.67.

## Rankings and availability

Alibaba's Qwen account said the model ranks 6th on Code Arena WebDev and gained 74 points over Qwen3.8-Max. The account also reported that the model beat Qwen3.8-Max on Agent Arena by a net margin of 7.5%. The model is available on OpenRouter and OpenCode Go. These results are based on the company's own statements.

Alibaba did not release the weights of Qwen4-Max. The company confirmed that smaller models in the Qwen4 series will be released as open source in the future.
