The U.S. Government Accountability Office (GAO) reported the results of its tests of Claude, Grok, ChatGPT and Gemini. The test team bypassed safety measures using known methods and obtained actionable information that could help an attack within 12 hours.

According to the report, at least one of the platforms provided information that would help evade detection and produce a very large bomb.

Senator Maggie Hassan reportedly plans a hearing on the issue in January.
