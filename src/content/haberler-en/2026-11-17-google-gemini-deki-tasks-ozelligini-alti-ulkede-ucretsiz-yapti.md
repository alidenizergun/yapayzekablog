Google made the Tasks feature in the Gemini app free for eligible users in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India on November 17, 2026. The feature previously required an AI Pro subscription. Google says the expansion, which runs on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model, gives access to hundreds of millions of people.

Advanced actions such as calls and payments are initially limited to the U.S. Users can switch between chat and task modes, and tasks run in a protected, isolated virtual machine.

Google's announcement did not specify usage limits, so "free" does not mean unlimited use.
