Wall Street lost more than 2% on November 19 as a sell-off in AI stocks weighed on the market. Japan and South Korea's exchanges fell more than 4% on the morning of November 20.

The BIST 100 index also opened November 20 down 1.22%, at 11,261.44 points. The opening decline showed that the sell-off had spread to Turkey's stock market.

According to Gedik Yatırım's assessment, concerns over the heavy investment spending and borrowing of AI companies deepened sell-offs across global markets, led by technology stocks. This assessment is a market commentary.

Concern over heavy spending is bringing the sector's financing debate back into focus.
