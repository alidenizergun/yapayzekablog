The Trump administration plans to name more than 24 AI companies, including Nvidia, Amazon, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic, as "national champions," the Wall Street Journal reported on Sunday (November 15). The report is based on sources familiar with the matter; no official statement has been made.

The aim of the plan is to help the companies expand abroad and compete better with Chinese rivals. The goal is for customers overseas to be able to buy US-made chips, models and other products as a single package.

The program was announced about a year ago, but the selected companies have not been disclosed until now. The plan is expected to be announced by Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick. The announcement is projected to come within a week; some sources say the timing could be delayed.
