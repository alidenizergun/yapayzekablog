IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva spoke to CNBC at the ASEAN summit in the Philippines. According to an IMF estimate, circular financing among AI companies, meaning deals in which invested money returns to the investor through purchase orders, has slightly exceeded $1 trillion. Two-thirds of it is financed by debt.

Georgieva said the global economy is standing "on one leg," and that leg is AI. She noted that investor enthusiasm has outpaced the capabilities the technology has demonstrated, and that some corrections are inevitable.

Georgieva described the recent market swings as "orderly" and also called for caution. CNBC reported that Nvidia has lost more than $1 trillion in market value over the past month.
