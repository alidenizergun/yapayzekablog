According to Reuters, Microsoft 365, Outlook and Teams were down for thousands of users in the U.S. on Thursday. The outage also affected the connectors used by OpenAI and Anthropic services. The Next Web reported that the outage lasted more than 12 hours, with the problem extending into Friday.

OpenAI said on its status page that new Microsoft connector connections could fail and that some tools might not work as expected. Anthropic said the issue initially stemmed from one of its service providers, then confirmed it was the Microsoft 365 outage.

Microsoft's Charles Lamanna said on X that there was no indication the outage was caused by a cyberattack or malicious activity. Microsoft said it would publish a detailed root cause report once the issue is fully resolved.
