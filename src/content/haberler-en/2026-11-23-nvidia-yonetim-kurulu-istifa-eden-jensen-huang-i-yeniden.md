Nvidia's board announced on Monday, November 23, that it had reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO and president. Huang had resigned after acknowledging at least two extramarital relationships, one of them with a woman the U.S. government suspected of being a Chinese spy. The board said it determined that Huang posed no national security risk after consulting senior U.S. government officials, and that the decision was reached unanimously.

The decision contradicts remarks made days earlier at the third-quarter earnings call, when board chair Harvey C. Jones said they were not considering bringing Huang back. Nvidia was also working with an executive search firm to find a permanent successor at the time. Interim CEO Colette Kress will continue to serve as chief financial officer.

President Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social that the board had made the right decision. Trump also said the Attorney General had confirmed there was no evidence that Huang had engaged in any illegal conduct. Nvidia shares opened 30% higher.
