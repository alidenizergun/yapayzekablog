Mistral AI announced on its official X account that the Vibe desktop app is now available. Users can assign multiple tasks within the app, and agents work on those tasks side by side. Outputs can be reviewed, and pull requests can be tracked from the same window.

The company also announced that it is offering Vibe Pro free for three months to developers in Europe. The offer is time-limited and runs on a first-come, first-served basis. For now, the information relies solely on Mistral's own posts.
