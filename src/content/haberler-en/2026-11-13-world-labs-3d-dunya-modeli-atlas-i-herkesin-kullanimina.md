Fei-Fei Li announced on X on November 12 that World Labs' Atlas world model is now available to everyone. World Labs unveiled Atlas on September 1, 2026; at that time, the model was in early access with select partners.

According to AlphaSignal, the model has moved beyond the stage where only select partners could use it; anyone can now sign up and use it directly. The launch includes two browser apps, Viewfinder and Chisel, as well as documented API. A real-time version is offered as a research preview and streams generated worlds from cloud GPUs to the browser at up to 15 frames per second. No separate pricing page has been published for Atlas.

According to World Labs, human evaluators preferred Atlas over competing video models 75% to 94% of the time in comparisons. However, the company has not published a paper or technical document (model card) on the model; these results have not been independently verified.
