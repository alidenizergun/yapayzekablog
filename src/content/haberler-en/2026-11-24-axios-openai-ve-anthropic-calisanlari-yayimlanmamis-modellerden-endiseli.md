According to Axios, which relied on more than two dozen employees, many staff at OpenAI and Anthropic no longer believe that unreleased frontier models can be safely controlled. The information comes from anonymous sources and has not been confirmed by the companies.

According to The Decoder, which covered the report, both companies are internally debating whether to halt the public release of these models entirely. Employees are reportedly worried that the systems are moving beyond human oversight.

The full Axios story could not be accessed, and The Decoder's article is also behind a paywall. No official response from the companies is included in the sources.
