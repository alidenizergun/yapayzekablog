According to Bloomberg's report dated November 19, 2026, Apple is developing its own autonomous AI agent that can perform tasks on users' behalf. The information is based on job postings added to the company's website this week.

One of the postings seeks a senior engineering manager for a proactive, agent-based AI assistant. The system is expected to write code, browse the web, control apps and move across devices. It will do all of this on the user's behalf.

OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta are also competing in the agent market. Apple's intention to enter this space has now come to light. However, there is no official product announcement yet; the information is based on a plan inferred from job postings.
