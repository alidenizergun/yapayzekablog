Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei sat down for a live conversation with JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon on November 13. According to Bloomberg, the discussion was part of the roadshow Anthropic is holding for investors ahead of its IPO.

Amodei said the company continues its work on security, safety and alignment, and that it does not want AI agents to spin out of control.

## Compute and the public benefit fund

According to FourWeekMBA, which published a transcript of the broadcast, Amodei described compute as the hardest business dilemma the company faces and said Anthropic is being cautious in its capacity purchases. Amodei also said the company has set aside 5% of its shares for a public benefit fund, as a safeguard against the concentration of wealth that AI could cause. For now, these statements rest solely on FourWeekMBA's account.

According to the same source, JPMorgan is a Claude customer in addition to being one of the underwriters on the IPO. Pricing is expected on November 18, with shares expected to begin trading on November 19.
