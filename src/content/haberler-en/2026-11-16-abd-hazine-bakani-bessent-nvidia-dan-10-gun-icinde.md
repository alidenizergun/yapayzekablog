U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said in a Fox News appearance on Sunday, November 15, that Nvidia should choose its next CEO from outside the company. Bessent said he expects a response within 10 days from Nvidia's board and its outside legal counsel.

Bessent questioned whether the company had pushed "seemingly illegal" sales to inflate its revenue and profit. The secretary also pointed to rising revenue recorded in Singapore while sales in China fell.

Nvidia will make its first major public statement since CEO Jensen Huang's resignation and CFO Colette Kress becoming interim CEO with its earnings report on Tuesday. How the company will address Bessent's questions in that statement remains to be seen.
