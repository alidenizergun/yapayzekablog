According to MLex, South Korea's Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources announced a roadmap for homegrown on-device AI chips at a meeting of the "Manufacturing AI Transformation Alliance." The ministry aims to secure the technology and production capacity for at least 10 types of AI chips by 2030 for autonomous vehicles, smart home appliances, robots and semiconductor design.

According to Digital Today, the roadmap also includes foundry cooperation with Samsung.

## Budget and date unclear

Sources give different figures for the project's budget: MLex cites $600 million, while Digital Today cites 800.2 billion won. The sources also differ on the date of the meeting: Digital Today says Wednesday, while MLex and Bytes Europe say November 12. The ministry has not yet issued an official statement with the figures.
