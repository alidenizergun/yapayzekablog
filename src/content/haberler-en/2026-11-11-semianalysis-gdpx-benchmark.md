Research firm SemiAnalysis introduced a new AI benchmark called GDPx on November 2, 2026. GDPx evaluates frontier models on agent tasks across 11 different economically valuable domains.

The benchmark focuses less on whether a model answers a single question correctly and more on whether it can complete multistep tasks from start to finish. The approach stands out as part of broader efforts to measure how AI agents translate to real-world business use.
