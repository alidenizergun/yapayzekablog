On November 13, Anthropic published a post signed by its engineering team titled "Building strong safety evaluations."

The recommendations cover the following areas: a test actually measuring the property it is meant to measure (construct validity), documenting the test method before results are seen (preregistration), validating automated scoring with human evaluators, multi-turn conversation simulations for sensitive requests, teams drawn from different disciplines, real-time monitoring and transparent reporting. Safety evaluations are recommended to be run in offline, sandboxed environments with real-time monitoring.

Anthropic also states that in single-turn tests, the harmless response rate of all its latest models is between 94% and 98%.
