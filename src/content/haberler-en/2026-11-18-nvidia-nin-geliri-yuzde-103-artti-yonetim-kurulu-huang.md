Nvidia reported third-quarter results after the market close on Tuesday, November 17, 2026. The company's revenue rose 103% year over year to $115.8 billion, and net income was recorded at $65.3 billion.

Data center revenue reached $107.6 billion, up 110% year over year. The company guided fourth-quarter revenue to a range of $127.4-132.6 billion. Nvidia also announced a $150 billion share buyback program.

The board said it will not bring back Jensen Huang, who resigned, as CEO. The company is currently run by interim CEO Colette Kress. The results are the first financial report released since the leadership change.
