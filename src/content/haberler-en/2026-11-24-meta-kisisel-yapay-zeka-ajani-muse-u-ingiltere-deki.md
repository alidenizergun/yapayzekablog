Meta made its personal AI agent Muse available to adult users in the UK on Tuesday, November 24. According to Politico, it is the first AI agent product from a Big Tech company offered in the UK. Meta says the product will help users with everyday tasks such as tracking refunds, renewing car insurance and holiday shopping.

Politico reports that regulators will be watching the rollout closely. In a statement last month, the Digital Regulation Cooperation Forum, made up of the Competition and Markets Authority (CMA), the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), Ofcom and the Information Commissioner's Office, warned that agent products could increase and accelerate consumer risks. According to Reuters, Meta employees raised concerns after the product's US launch about unwanted actions, including access to encrypted messages and providing false information about purchases. A Meta spokesperson described these as isolated incidents.

In a blog post, Meta said Muse is private, secure and safe by design. Users can choose whether their interactions are used to improve Meta's AI, but they must change the setting themselves to opt out. Muse had begun [calling businesses in the US](https://www.yzgundemi.com/haber/meta-nin-yapay-zeka-ajani-muse-ucretli-kullanicilar-adina/) on November 13.
