OpenAI announced GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol on November 17, 2026. The announcement came a week after the release of Anthropic's Claude 5.2 and Google's Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Astra is a frontier model designed for the most demanding projects, while Sol is presented as an efficient, versatile model for everyday tasks.

The models are available to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex. In the API, they can be accessed as gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol. Astra's standard price is $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens; for Sol, those amounts are $2 and $10, respectively. Astra's prices have been cut by 20% compared with the previous model.

Astra is the first model rated "high" for self-improvement risk. OpenAI also said it is releasing the models with stricter safeguards against high-risk cybersecurity uses due to their growing capabilities.
