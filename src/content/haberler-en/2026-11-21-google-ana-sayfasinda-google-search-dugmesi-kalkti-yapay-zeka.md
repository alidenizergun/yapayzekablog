Google's search vice president Robby Stein announced on X on November 19 that the new design of the google.com desktop homepage was rolling out globally. In a November 20 report, Ars Technica said the design, which had been tested for several weeks, is now officially live. The "Google Search" button below the search bar has been removed; the "I'm Feeling Lucky" button remains. It has been replaced by shortcuts for image generation, file analysis and idea generation with Gemini.

Users signed in with a Google account see a collapsible sidebar on the left showing recent AI Mode chats. The panel is also accessible from the search results page. Stein said that during testing, the panel made a "big difference" in how users move through a search.

According to Digital Trends, the change applies only to desktop and is rolling out gradually; it may take days or even weeks before it appears for everyone. There is no statement on whether Turkey is included.
