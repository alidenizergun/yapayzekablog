AI-driven drug design company Isomorphic Labs announced a $5 billion Series C funding round on November 17, 2026. Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital co-led the round, with Andreessen Horowitz and several new investors also participating. Alphabet reportedly continued to invest in the round.

The round followed the $2.1 billion Series B in May. The company did not disclose its new valuation; Bloomberg had previously reported that the company was seeking funding at a $50 billion valuation target.

The company's plan to move its drug candidates into clinical trials stands out as the main purpose of the funding. The round is considered the largest financing tracked since 2022.
