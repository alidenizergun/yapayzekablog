China's Tencent on Monday announced Hy4 flash, a low-cost model designed for everyday tasks such as research, slide preparation and spreadsheet editing. The model has 224 billion parameters and activates 12 billion of them on each pass. It supports multimodal input and a context window of up to one million tokens.

The API list price is $0.07 per million tokens for input and $0.281 for output. On Tencent's own WorkIndex measure, the cost per task is one-twentieth that of the full Hy4 model. The company says these comparisons are based on internal tests.

The model is available in WorkBuddy and Yuanbao, as well as in Tencent Cloud's TokenHub service. Tencent plans to release the model weights and offer the model on OpenRouter.
