The UK Parliament's Business, Innovation, Science and Trade Committee published its report, "Guardrails for growth: making AI safe." The report was covered by Computer Weekly on November 18, 2026.

According to the report, AI companies should be required to prove their safety claims. Independent experts should be able to examine these claims, and a regulator should have the power to intervene where risks cannot be controlled.

The report also warns that Chinese developers are producing a growing number of open-weight models and that these are difficult to control once they are released.

Former Anthropic and OpenAI researchers were among those who gave evidence to the committee. They told lawmakers that leading model developers are not taking AI risks seriously enough.
