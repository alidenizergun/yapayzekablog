OpenAI announced ChatGPT Wallet on Friday, November 13, a product that lets its AI agents, which it calls "dots," make purchases on behalf of users. The product was developed with American Express, Mastercard and Visa. OpenAI President Greg Brockman also announced the product and the partnership with the payment networks on his X account.

Here is how the system works: The user enters card details once, asks a dot to find a product, and approves each purchase with a security step. According to OpenAI, the agent never sees card details; the payment networks generate a single-use code (payment token) that stands in for the card in each transaction. Cardholders continue to benefit from their cards' existing perks, such as rewards points and purchase protection.

The feature is initially available only to Pro subscribers in the US who are 18 or older. OpenAI is not taking a cut of transactions at launch. The company says that in the future, users will be able to pre-approve purchases within certain parameters and hand them over entirely to the agent.

The details in this report are based on Brockman's post and on reports by CNBC and 9to5Mac.
