The Justice Ministry is preparing to add a new regulation to the 13th Judicial Package as part of its efforts to combat crimes committed through artificial intelligence. According to the draft, creating fake images and audio recordings with AI without a person's consent would be directly classified as a crime.

Requiring AI-generated content to be labeled is also on the agenda. Whether to impose age limits on the use of AI tools is also being debated.

In related reporting, Justice Ministry official Oğuz Demir is reported to have noted that France, England and Italy have already made deepfake content a standalone crime.

The regulation is still at the draft stage; the final text and effective date have not been determined.
