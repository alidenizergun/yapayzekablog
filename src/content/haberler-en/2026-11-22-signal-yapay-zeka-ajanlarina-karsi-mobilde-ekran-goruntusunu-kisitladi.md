Encrypted messaging app Signal announced on Friday that it has limited screenshot capture in its Android and iOS versions. Signal President Meredith Whittaker said that allowing Windows, Android and macOS/iOS to give AI agents the ability to "scan" on-screen information undermines Signal's ability to independently guarantee privacy. According to Whittaker, once data is captured it leaves Signal's control, and the privacy of all parties in a conversation is put at risk.

According to Notebookcheck, on iPhones running iOS 27, the "Block Siri AI" setting is on by default in Signal 8.37 and later. According to Digital Trends, on Android, Signal automatically turns on its "Screen Security" feature when it detects that an AI assistant is trying to read the screen. Users can turn the setting off; however, according to TechCrunch, Whittaker said doing so would break privacy guarantees. Whittaker also acknowledged that the solution is "incomplete." Signal already blocked screenshots by default in its Windows app.
