Ethan Mollick announced on X that Wharton Generative AI Labs has released Primer. According to Mollick, a teacher describes their lesson to the system and gets back a simulation that students can experience. The project's paper, prompts and code are publicly available.

The paper, published on arXiv, describes Primer as an open-source system that helps educators create simulations adaptable to each student and works with any language model. Educators prepare the simulation by conversing with AI, without writing code.

The system works in two stages. In the "preparation" stage, the educator designs the simulation together with AI agents. In the "delivery" stage, the student experiences the simulation.

The team says Primer is an experimental prototype, not a product that has been tested in classrooms.
