Following the resignation of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, a petition calling for his return continues to grow. According to a report by the South China Morning Post (SCMP), the campaign had reached more than 136,000 signatures as of 2:00 p.m. on Sunday, November 15. South Korea's Financial News also cites roughly 136,000 participants.

According to the SCMP, signatories include more than 6,800 people who identify themselves as Nvidia employees, 73,000 who say they are shareholders, and Elon Musk. Because signatures are collected on a self-reported basis, these figures have not been verified.

## Statement from Megaspeed

Singapore-based Megaspeed International, which is alleged to have supplied Nvidia chips to Chinese companies, issued a statement on Sunday. According to the company, Alice Huang, the executive at the center of the controversy, left the company in August 2025. The statement did not address whether Alice Huang retains control over the company through her shares. Megaspeed also stressed that it has not engaged in any illegal activity.

## Background and the board

In a letter sent to the board on Friday, November 13, Brad Gerstner of investment firm Altimeter and Gavin Baker of Atreides had asked that the search for a new CEO not be rushed and that the possibility of Huang returning also be considered. The board, for its part, said it has selected a consulting firm for the CEO search but has not yet held formal talks with any candidates.

Darrell Dai, a robotics engineer at Nvidia, also announced on X that he is leaving the company. Dai wrote that his reason for leaving was Huang's departure and that engineers' views were not sought during the process.
