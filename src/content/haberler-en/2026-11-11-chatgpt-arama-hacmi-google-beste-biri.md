OpenAI announced that search volume on ChatGPT has reached roughly one-fifth of the search volume on Google. The figure suggests that chat-based AI assistants are no longer a side channel for accessing information but one of the main channels.

The announcement comes as the impact of AI answers on the news ecosystem is being debated in Europe. Publishers argue that AI-generated answers can replace news content without royalties being paid. Google, for its part, points to existing legal exceptions and the control tools it offers publishers.

The view put forward so far in the European debate is not binding. The final decision is expected to shape the relationship between publishers and AI companies, as well as potential licensing deals.
