Foreign investors have posted record outflows from Taiwan stocks so far in November. According to data compiled by Bloomberg, net selling through the date reached NT$1.09 trillion, or $33.1 billion.

The amount surpasses the previous record of NT$994 billion set in October. Bloomberg notes that Taiwan's technology stocks have taken a heavy hit, with Taiwan seen as a leading indicator for the global AI sector.

The figure is not final because the month has not yet ended.
