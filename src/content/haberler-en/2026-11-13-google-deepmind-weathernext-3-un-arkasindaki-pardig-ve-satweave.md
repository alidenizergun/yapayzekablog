Google DeepMind released the SatWeave and PARDIG datasets on November 13. The datasets are aimed at developers building weather, hydrology and climate applications; both underpin the recently released WeatherNext 3 model.

PARDIG provides global precipitation estimates at 15-minute intervals and roughly 10-kilometer resolution. SatWeave, meanwhile, is updated every hour. As a result, WeatherNext 3 can refresh its global forecasts far more often than models that run on data updated every six hours. According to DeepMind's post on X, PARDIG improves precipitation detection by more than 30%.

The data can be accessed through Google Cloud Storage; PARDIG's historical catalogs are also available in Earth Engine. According to the arXiv preprint, the PARDIG datasets are openly available under a CC BY 4.0 license.
