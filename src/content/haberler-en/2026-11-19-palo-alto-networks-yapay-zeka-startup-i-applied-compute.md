Palo Alto Networks has agreed to acquire San Francisco-based AI startup Applied Compute for $4.5 billion, paid entirely in stock. The deal was announced on the day the company reported its quarterly results.

Applied Compute was founded in 2025 by former OpenAI researchers. The company offers a platform for post-training and inference (inference) of open-weight models. Before the acquisition, it had raised more than $500 million in funding.

Once the deal closes, Applied Compute's co-founder and CEO Yash Patil will become co-chief AI officer at Palo Alto. The startup's research team will also join Palo Alto.

## Results also beat expectations

Palo Alto reported revenue of $3.45 billion on the same day. The figure exceeded the $3.31 billion analysts had expected. The company's shares rose more than 8% in after-hours trading.
