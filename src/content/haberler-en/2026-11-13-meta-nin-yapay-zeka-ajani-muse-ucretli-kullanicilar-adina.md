Meta's consumer AI agent Muse can call businesses in the U.S. on users' behalf. The feature was rolled out after an initial test in September and is open only to paid subscribers.

Subscription plans start at $20 a month for 500 million Muse tokens per week. A $100-a-month plan with 3 billion tokens is also available for heavy users.

Meta's chief AI officer Alexandr Wang said the feature can be used for places people would rather not call, such as a cable company, the DMV (the driver's license and vehicle registration agency), or restaurants that don't accept online reservations. According to Wang, the ability to give Muse its own email address will also soon be available to free users.

Meanwhile, some businesses and receptionists hang up when they realize the caller is an AI.
