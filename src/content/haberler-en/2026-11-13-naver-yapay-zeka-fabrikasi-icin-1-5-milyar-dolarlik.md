Naver has signed a computing services contract worth 2 trillion won ($1.5 billion) with an undisclosed U.S. AI inference company. Naver announced the deal during the call at which it reported its third-quarter financial results.

The contract covers roughly 20 MW of capacity for four to five years. The capacity consists of a mix of NVIDIA B300 and Vera Rubin GPUs.

Naver's AI factory project, which it is running jointly with Brookfield and Nvidia, is planned to start with 55 MW in the first half of 2027 and scale up to 200 MW by 2028. Naver is in talks with more than 10 customers from outside Asia, including large model developers, for the remaining 180 MW.
