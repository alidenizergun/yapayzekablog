Bloomberg, citing sources familiar with the matter, reported that Anthropic's IPO has drawn orders for roughly six times the shares on offer. The company did not confirm the figure. Bloomberg had reported a day earlier that demand had reached several times the size of the offering.

Anthropic and its founders aim to raise up to $64.4 billion from the IPO and up to $26 billion from a concurrent private sale. Taken together, the two transactions could make Anthropic's IPO the largest in history, surpassing SpaceX.

Pricing is expected around November 18, with trading set to begin on the Nasdaq on November 19 under the ticker "ANT." According to the Financial Times, banks are preparing to raise the valuation to $1.5 trillion.
