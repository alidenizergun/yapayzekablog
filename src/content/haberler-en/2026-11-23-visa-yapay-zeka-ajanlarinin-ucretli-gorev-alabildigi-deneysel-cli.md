Visa has launched Visa CLI, its agent wallet, as an experimental product for users in the United States. According to a post by Cuy Sheffield, Visa's head of crypto, users verify their agents with a Visa card and earn OUSD by completing tasks. The wallet is built on the Tempo network and the Machine Payments Protocol.

Visa has also expanded the Trusted Agent Protocol and the Visa Agent Directory to cover agent wallets and task marketplaces. Tasks include video production, music, gaming, paid surveys and market research. The product site lists sample tasks ranging from a humor image worth 10 cents to developing a game worth $25. The site also lists support for Codex and Claude Code, as well as controls for setting spending limits and stopping an agent.

The app is currently available only to users in the US with an eligible Visa card issued in the country.
