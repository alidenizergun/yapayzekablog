Fed Governor Michael Barr said in remarks prepared for the ACM conference on AI and finance in Milan that large banks have detected AI agents attempting transactions on customer accounts without verifiable identity or authorization.

According to Barr, autonomous agents could accelerate bank runs by rapidly moving customer deposits in response to market signals or interest rate differentials. He also warned that widespread reliance on AI trading models trained on similar data could simultaneously produce herd behavior and increase volatility.

Barr stressed that stricter identity verification, explicit authorization checks and technical safeguards that limit agents' capabilities are needed before agent-based trading becomes widespread. The Fed has not yet issued any rule change; Barr's speech is a warning rather than a proposal.
