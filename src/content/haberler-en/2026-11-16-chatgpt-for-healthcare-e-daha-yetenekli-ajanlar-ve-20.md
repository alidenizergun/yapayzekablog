OpenAI announced that ChatGPT for Healthcare has been expanded with more capable computer-using agents, stronger clinical responses and more than 20 new health plugins.

Organizations including Abridge, Ambience Healthcare, Cigna and CVS Health are using the models for prior authorization, referrals, discharge planning and patient communication.

The company said it aims to connect the models' medical evidence with the everyday tools that clinicians and staff already use.
