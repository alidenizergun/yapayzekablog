Nvidia shares lost more than 8% after the market close on Wednesday following reports that the board was considering removing CEO Jensen Huang. The drop is based on a Wall Street Journal report. Reuters, citing a source, reported that the board met Wednesday evening to discuss whether to remove Huang. As of Thursday morning, no decision had been announced.

Nvidia has not yet announced a decision; there is no formal removal, only a review reportedly under way within the board.

## The board's authority and the investigation

Under the company's bylaws, a majority of the 11-member board has the authority to remove Huang "with or without cause." A day earlier, the board had also launched an independent investigation following reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort.

The reports also include allegations about Huang's private life; these have not been confirmed by the company. Separately, Bernstein cut its price target for Nvidia on Thursday from $400 to $200.
