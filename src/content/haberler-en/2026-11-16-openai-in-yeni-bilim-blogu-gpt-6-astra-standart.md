OpenAI has launched its new science blog. The first guest post, dated November 15, 2026, was written by physicist Nathaniel Craig, a visiting scientist at OpenAI. Craig describes how he worked with GPT-6 Astra on a candidate lattice definition of the Standard Model.

Craig is a particle theorist at the University of California, Santa Barbara. In an arXiv preprint published in October, he proposed a candidate definition for the Standard Model. He says Astra took the lead in the detailed proof.

According to Craig, GPT-6 Astra was decisive in turning the refinement idea into a working construction. He also says the corresponding question for the full Standard Model has been open for more than 25 years.

The result is presented not as a settled solution but as a "candidate" definition, and it has not yet been verified by independent experts. The post was announced from OpenAI's X account as the first entry on the new science blog; OpenAI's Greg Brockman also shared it.
