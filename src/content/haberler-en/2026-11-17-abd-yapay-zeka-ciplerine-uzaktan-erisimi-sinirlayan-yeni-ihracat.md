The U.S. Department of Commerce has issued a new rule aimed at preventing circumvention of export restrictions on advanced AI chips and servers through remote access via third countries. The development was announced on November 17, 2026.

According to Investing.com, data center operators in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil will need to obtain licenses to export advanced AI chips. The report also says the rule pulled down Asian stock exchanges.

The regulation expands chip access restrictions beyond physical exports alone, covering remote access as well.
